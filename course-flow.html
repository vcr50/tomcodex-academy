TomCodeX LEITR study system

Learn > Explain > Implement > Test > Review

Use this rhythm in every Flow mastery step. Keep learning short, implementation heavy, and review spaced over time. For every 1 hour learning, spend at least 2 hours implementing.

1. Learn

25 minutes: study one Flow concept only.

2. Explain

10 minutes: answer what, why, when, and one example.

3. Implement

30-60 minutes: build it yourself in Salesforce.

4. Test

10 minutes: explain, rebuild, and answer an interview question.

5. Review (Functional Reminder)

Review after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days.
